# Missing and incomplete features

## Priority P0: security and financial integrity

- Verified payment provider integration with webhook signature validation
- Provider-side payment idempotency and deduplication for checkout and refunds
- Explicit inventory reservation and release rules for failed/cancelled checkouts
- Clear separation of payment status, fulfillment status, and order status
- Tenant isolation proof across every commerce collection and admin route
- Platform billing enforcement by plan, storage limits, and staff limits

## Priority P1: core merchant operations

- Full store suspension / reactivation flows and retention policy enforcement
- Custom domain + SSL / verification lifecycle automation
- More complete store staff role permissions and invitation acceptance controls
- Product publishing and archive safety checks
- Customer export / deletion policy controls and privacy compliance flows
- Usable admin and merchant analytics tied to reliable financial events

## Priority P2: commerce experience and scale

- Better theme versioning and publishing control
- More complete multi-store switching UX and plan entitlement checks
- Broader discount validation, stacking rules, and usage tracking integrity
- Search, collection, and filter quality improvements
- Deeper storefront SEO and system health reporting

## Priority P3: advanced or optional Shopify-style capability

- Stronger marketplace or channel integrations
- More robust automation around order notifications, subscriptions, and retention
- Advanced fulfillment carrier integration and tracking workflows
- More complete support tools for platform staff and merchant support operations

## Most important missing features to close next

1. Payment provider integration and verification
2. Safe inventory reservation lifecycle
3. Completed invoice + subscription enforcement
4. Multi-tenant enforcement across all store-scoped actions
5. Refund and return financial reconciliation
